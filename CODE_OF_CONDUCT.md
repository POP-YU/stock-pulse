# Community Conduct

StockPulse welcomes contributors with different experience levels and backgrounds. Discussions should stay focused on improving the project and should be respectful, specific, and technically constructive.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful when disagreeing about code, design, or project direction.
- Critique ideas and changes, not people.
- Give contributors enough context to understand requested changes.
- Assume good intent while still being clear about security, correctness, and scope concerns.
- Keep issue and pull request discussions relevant to the project.
- Respect privacy; never pressure others to share credentials, financial-account information, or personal data.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, targeted insults, deliberate disclosure of private information, spam, and repeated disruption of project spaces are not acceptable.

## Moderation

The maintainer may edit, hide, lock, or remove content that violates these guidelines and may restrict participation when necessary to keep the project safe and productive.

For sensitive conduct concerns, contact the maintainer through the GitHub profile at `https://github.com/POP-YU` and request a private channel rather than posting personal details publicly.

These guidelines apply to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project-controlled spaces.
